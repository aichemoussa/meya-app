# Security policy

MEYA is a private messenger, so security reports matter a great deal to us.
Thank you for helping keep people safe.

## Reporting a problem

Please report vulnerabilities **privately**. Do not open a public issue or
discuss the problem publicly until it has been fixed.

Use GitHub's private reporting: open this repository's **Security** tab and
choose **Report a vulnerability**.

Please include what you found, the version (or commit) it affects, how to
reproduce it, and what you think the impact is. We will do our best to
acknowledge your report promptly, keep you informed, and credit you when the
fix is published, if you wish.

## What we are most interested in

- Ways to read, change or forge messages, media, calls or contact identities.
- Weaknesses in how keys are created, stored, exchanged or verified.
- Ways to read another person's data from the relay or the backend, or to
  impersonate another device.
- Ways to extract data from the app's local storage or backups.
- Mistakes in the cryptographic design or in how libsignal is used.

## Good-faith research

We will not take action against people who:

- test only on their own accounts and devices;
- do not access, change or share other people's data;
- do not disrupt the service for others (no denial-of-service or spam);
- report in good faith and give us reasonable time to fix the problem before
  going public.

## Supported versions

MEYA is in testing. Fixes are made on the latest version, which is the newest
release.
