file name shown. JPG or PNG, at least 1200 px on the long side. They will be
cropped, compressed and placed in the app showcase on the home page.

Use the same **style block** at the end of every prompt so all images look
like one photo shoot.

> Photorealistic lifestyle photograph, natural window light, warm and soft

> home interior with light neutral walls and subtle touches of deep blue
> (#044D8C), no text, no logos, no brand names on devices, phone screen not
> visible to the camera, respectful and dignified, high detail, 50mm lens.
