# Photo prompts: birthday and grandparents

These replace the last two stock photos on the website, so every person shown
is AI generated and nobody can recognise themselves.

Save the results as JPG or PNG, at least 1200 px on the long side, and add
them to the website. They will be resized into `website/img/ai/`.

Use the same style block as the other photos so they match:

> Photorealistic lifestyle photograph, natural light, warm and soft colours,
> shallow depth of field, genuine happy expressions, subtle touches of deep
> blue (#044D8C), no text, no logos, no brand names on devices or packaging,
> phone screens not visible to the camera, respectful and dignified, high
> detail, 50mm lens.

small round cake with lit candles and is about to blow them out. Paper cups,
fruit and a few balloons in soft blue and white around them. Faces turned
towards the camera and clearly visible, all heads in the upper half of the
frame, a little empty space above their heads. Golden afternoon light,
blurred green trees behind.

Used for: the "Happy birthday!" photo bubble and the round "Friends" chat
picture. Keep the cake and faces near the centre so both crops work.
