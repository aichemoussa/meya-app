How it works

No phone number. No address book upload. No public directory.

  1. Create your space

    Choose a name and a passcode. Your account lives on your phone.

  2. Meet and scan

    Add someone by scanning their MEYA QR code in person. Strangers simply can't find you.

  3. Talk freely

    Messages, calls and media are encrypted on your phone and opened only on theirs.

Encryption

Three locks, braided together

Every message gets its own fresh key. MEYA mixes keys from three different methods, so an attacker would have to break all of them at once. One of them is built to resist future quantum computers.

We use Signal's own open-source code, unchanged. We don't write our own cryptography.

  • A new key for every message
  • Recovers safety if a key is ever exposed
  • Protects against future quantum computers

Private by design

MEYA uses proven, open-source end-to-end encryption that is built to resist future quantum computers. Here is exactly what happens to your data.

Only you and them

  • Your messages, voice messages, photos, videos and files
  • Your calls, profile, status and planned calls
  • Your contacts and who you are connected with
  • Everything MEYA keeps on your phone, encrypted and left out of cloud backups

What our servers can see

  • A random device ID for each phone, with no name or number
  • Which device sent a sealed envelope to which, when, and how big
  • Your network address while you are connected
  • Never the content: envelopes are sealed, and deleted once delivered

No ads. No analytics or trackers. We don't sell or share data. MEYA is open source under the AGPL-3.0 licence. Read the privacy policy.
